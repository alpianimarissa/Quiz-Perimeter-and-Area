Geometry All Around Us!

Inquiry into how shapes build our physical world

🔲

Square

4 equal sides, 90° corners

  • ✨ Floor tiles & ceramics
  • ✨ Chessboard layout
  • ✨ Sticky notes
📱

Rectangle

Opposite sides equal

  • ✨ Smartphone & TV screen
  • ✨ Classroom whiteboard
  • ✨ Swimming pool layout
🍕

Triangle

3 sides and 3 angles

  • ✨ Roof truss structures
  • ✨ Pizza slices & nachos
  • ✨ Warning road signs
🏬

Parallelogram

Slanted opposite sides

  • ✨ Modern architectural facades
  • ✨ Unique parking lot stalls
  • ✨ Cultural textile patterns
👜

Trapezoid

One pair of parallel sides

  • ✨ Handbag & purse designs
  • ✨ Bridges & beam supports
  • ✨ Lamp shade structures
💎

Rhombus

4 equal sides, slanted

  • ✨ Diamond gemstone cut
  • ✨ Baseball infield dirt shape
  • ✨ Window glass mosaics
🪁

Kite

2 pairs of adjacent sides

  • ✨ Flying park toys (kites)
  • ✨ Jewelry pendants
  • ✨ Decorative ceiling tiles
⏰

Circle

Equidistant center point

  • ✨ Clocks & watches
  • ✨ Bicycles wheels & gears
  • ✨ Circular running tracks
💡

IB Connection: Understanding area allows interior designers to calculate how much flooring material or paint is needed for a room remodel!
